# Directing Review Guardrails

Use this for critiques of directing statements, shot lists, scene plans, actor notes, and analysis drafts.

## Findings To Raise

| Problem | Why it fails under Dancyger | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Idea is only plot summary | Plot tells what happens; directing must state what the story means underneath the action. | Rewrite as a subtextual pressure on character and audience. |
| Idea is a broad theme | Themes such as love, violence, freedom, or loneliness do not yet guide choices. | Specify the form: doomed love, violence in ordinary life, freedom as illusion, loneliness as enclosed existence. |
| Technique leads interpretation | Camera style, lighting, or editing is chosen before the idea is known. | Derive craft choices from text interpretation and performance needs. |
| Actor plan lacks arc | Performance cannot build if the character's emotional road map is unclear. | Define starting state, pressure, turns, and ending state. |
| Camera plan lacks relation | Shots record action but do not position the viewer morally, emotionally, or psychologically. | Decide objective/subjective stance, proximity, height, movement, and rhythm. |
| Case study is copied literally | A director's style belongs to a specific idea and story pressure. | Borrow the decision logic, not the surface style. |
| Greatness is asserted | The book's hierarchy is analytic, not promotional. | Show how interpretation, actor work, and camera combine into distinctive voice. |

## Severity Scale

- High: no director's idea, or craft choices contradict the stated idea.
- Medium: plausible idea, but one channel such as actor, camera, or text interpretation is underdeveloped.
- Low: idea and channels align, but source grounding, examples, or wording are weak.

## Review Procedure

1. Quote or restate the user's proposed idea.
2. Classify it: plot summary, theme, mood, subtextual idea, or craft tactic.
3. Test actor and camera consequences.
4. Compare to the nearest book framework or director case.
5. Give one concrete revision, not a list of unrelated possibilities.
